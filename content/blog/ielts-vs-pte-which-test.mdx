Both IELTS and PTE Academic are widely accepted for study in the UK, Australia and New Zealand. Choose the test your university **and** your visa route accept, then pick the format you are most comfortable with: IELTS has a face-to-face speaking test, while PTE is fully computer-based with faster results.

## What is the main difference between IELTS and PTE?

IELTS Academic tests Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, and the Speaking test is a conversation with an examiner. PTE Academic is taken entirely on a computer, and your speaking is recorded and scored automatically.

| | IELTS Academic | PTE Academic |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Paper or computer; face-to-face speaking | Fully computer-based |
| Score scale | Bands 0–9 | 10–90 |
| Results | Usually within a few days to two weeks | Usually within about two days |

## Which test is easier for Nepali students?

Neither test is "easier". It depends on your strengths. Students who are confident speaking with a person often prefer IELTS. Students who type quickly and are comfortable with computer-based tasks often prefer PTE.

## Which universities and visas accept each test?

Most universities in our destinations accept both, but requirements differ by course. Some visa routes require a specific version of a test (for example, IELTS for UKVI for certain UK applications). Always confirm with your university and check the official visa page before booking.

## How do I decide?

1. Check your university's English requirement for your exact course.
2. Check whether your visa route names a specific test.
3. Take a free mock test in both formats and compare your scores.
