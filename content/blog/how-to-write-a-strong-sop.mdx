A strong Statement of Purpose explains, in your own words, why you chose this course and university, how it builds on your past study or work, and what you plan to do afterwards. Admissions teams and visa officers look for honesty, a clear plan and genuine motivation — not fancy language.

## What should a Statement of Purpose include?

1. **Your background** — your studies, results and any work experience.
2. **Why this course** — specific modules or skills you want to gain.
3. **Why this university and country** — concrete reasons, not generic praise.
4. **Your career plan** — what you will do after graduating and how the course helps.
5. **Gaps or weak points** — explain them briefly and honestly.

## How long should an SOP be?

Follow the university's word limit exactly. If none is given, 800–1,000 words is a common length for postgraduate applications.

## What mistakes should I avoid?

- Copying templates or another student's SOP — universities use plagiarism checks.
- Listing achievements without explaining why they matter.
- Contradicting your financial or visa documents.
