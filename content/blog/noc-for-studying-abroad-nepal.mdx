A No Objection Certificate (NOC) is a letter from Nepal's Ministry of Education, Science and Technology confirming that you are going abroad for study. Students usually need it so banks in Nepal can transfer foreign currency for tuition and living costs. Apply after you receive your offer or enrolment document.

## Why do Nepali students need an NOC?

Banks and financial institutions in Nepal generally ask for an NOC before transferring money abroad for education. Without it, you may not be able to pay your tuition deposit or show transferable funds.

## When should I apply for the NOC?

Apply once you have an offer letter or enrolment document (such as a CAS, CoE, I-20 or COE) and know the fees you need to pay. Leave enough time before your deposit deadline.

## What documents are usually required?

- Passport
- Academic certificates and transcripts
- Offer letter or enrolment document from the institution
- Evidence of fees to be paid
- Any additional documents requested by the Ministry at the time of application
